## Repository-Wide Capability Audit (Prompts 1–13, Non-ERP Scope)

| # | Capability Area | Existing Repository Evidence Inspected | Pre-Upgrade Status | Genuine Non-ERP Gap Identified |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Canonical Invoice Lifecycle & Exception Engine (Prompt 2)** | `approval_workflow/app/services/workflow_service.py`, `human_review/app/services/review_case_service.py` | `partially implemented` | Existing services track local statuses (`pending_approval`, `held_by_controls`, etc.), but lack a unified 22-state canonical lifecycle state machine (`RECEIVED` -> ... -> `COMPLETED`) with optimistic-locked transitions, all 12 classified exception queues/remediations, and checkpoint reprocessing that invalidates prior `APPROVED` state upon material financial change. |
| 2 | **Financial Invariants, Policy Lifecycle & GL Dimension Validation (Prompt 3)** | `three_way_matching/app/services/financial_policy_governance.py`, `approval_workflow/app/services/gl_coding_engine.py` | `partially implemented` | Pure `Decimal` math, currency minor units, UOM, GST/IGST/TDS, and `403` prod draft block exist, but policies are in-memory rather than DB-versioned (`draft -> test_evaluated -> approved` with `author != approver`), line/header discount & declared-vs-calculated total invariants need explicit non-mutating reconciliation flags, and configurable GL account/cost-center/tax-code dimension rules need explicit enforcement. |
| 3 | **Route-by-Route Authorization, Tenant Isolation & Approval Governance (Prompt 4)** | `security_auditability/app/core/permissions.py`, `authorization_policy_service.py` | `implemented` & `locally tested` (`partially implemented` route matrix export) | RBAC, ABAC, SoD, MFA, and tenant checks are implemented; needs an explicit machine-readable & documented route-by-route permission matrix (`GET /api/v1/iam/route-permission-matrix`) with data classification, financial-state flag, step-up MFA requirement, and cross-tenant negative tests across lineage/overrides/exports. |
| 4 | **Document Quality Detection, Provenance & Privacy-Gated Corrections (Prompt 5)** | `document_ingestion/app/services/{image_preprocessor,pdf_extractor}.py`, `intelligent_extraction/app/services/confidence_engine.py` | `partially implemented` | Deskew/DPI normalization and per-field confidence exist, but lacks a dedicated `DocumentQualityInspector` classifying password-protected PDFs, Laplacian blur, skew/rotation, empty pages, missing page-number sequences (`Page 1 of 3` -> `Page 3 of 3`), invalid dimensions, decompression pixel bombs, and an opt-in privacy-governed training-feedback exporter. |
| 5 | **Governed Fraud/Duplicate Intelligence & Alert Delivery Durability (Prompt 6)** | `fraud_detection/app/services/fraud_detection/{alert_management,explanations}.py`, `testing_monitoring/app/evaluation/splitters.py` | `partially implemented` (`blocked` on real customer corpus) | Multi-detector separation, XAI attributions, val-only tuning, and PSI drift exist; needs durable alert notification delivery tracking (`delivery_status`, `delivery_attempts`, `last_error`) so failed webhook/email notifications never lose a fraud alert, plus structured `recommended_investigation_steps` and explicit disposition states (`suspicious`, `false_positive`, `unresolved`, `confirmed_fraud`). |
| 6 | **12-Point Decision Lineage & Governed Manual Override Ledger (Prompt 7)** | `security_auditability/app/services/audit_integrity_service.py`, `erp_integration/app/models.py` | `partially implemented` | Keyed-HMAC append-only audit chain exists, but lacks a unified 12-point per-invoice Decision Lineage reconstructor (using redacted references) and a dedicated append-only Manual Override Ledger requiring structured reason codes, original vs overridden values, model/rule versions, and independent secondary approval. |
| 7 | **Durable Background Jobs, Heartbeat, Cancellation & Backpressure (Prompt 8)** | `document_ingestion/app/tasks/outbox_worker.py` | `partially implemented` | Atomic SQLite lease, crash recovery, backoff, poison-message DLQ, and RBAC replay exist; needs job `schema_version`, `priority` ordering, worker `heartbeat_lease`, `detect_stuck_jobs`, `cancel_job` semantics, and `max_queue_depth` backpressure rejection (`429`/`QueueBackpressureError`). |
| 8 | **Provisional SLOs, Actionable Alerts, p50/p95/p99 Load & Incident Drills (Prompt 9)** | `testing_monitoring/app/observability/`, `scripts/run_dr_backup_restore_drill.py` | `partially implemented` (`blocked` on live PagerDuty/SMTP & prod infra) | Prometheus metrics, trace correlation, and encrypted restore drill (`2.639s` RTO) exist; needs a formal Provisional SLO Registry (`PROVISIONAL_PENDING_OWNER_APPROVAL` across all 10 SLO dimensions), a reproducible p50/p95/p99 load/concurrency harness, and simulated corrupted-backup/expired-key/compromised-credential drill tests. |
| 9 | **Data Privacy Classification, Legal-Hold Retention & Training Consent (Prompt 10)** | `security_auditability/app/services/data_protection_service.py` | `partially implemented` | AES-256-GCM bank encryption & log redaction exist; needs a complete 8-category Data Classification Inventory, a Legal-Hold & Financial/Audit Retention Enforcer (blocking upload deletion from erasing required financial/audit records), audited redacted data export, and training-data consent enforcement. |
| 10 | **API Contracts, Real Frontend Controls & Root Packaging Tests (Prompts 11 & 12)** | `unified_portal/app/{main,orchestrator,ui}.py`, `scripts/{run_all_tests,build_clean_archive}.py` | `partially implemented` | Health/readiness and 6-tab portal exist; needs interactive frontend-to-backend controls for Lifecycle Recovery, 12-Point Decision Lineage, and Governed Overrides (with RBAC, validation, loading/error states, and audit trails), commit/env metadata in `run_all_tests.py`, and an automated test suite inspecting actual `.tar.gz`/`.zip` archive members. |
| 11 | **Optional AI Enhancements (`LayoutLMv3`, `Neo4j`, Contract LLM) (Prompt 13)** | `intelligent_extraction/app/extractors/llm_assisted.py`, `relationship_analysis/.../neo4j_client.py` | `locally tested` guardrails / `blocked` on real corpus & Neo4j cluster | Requires a formal `docs/OPTIONAL_AI_FEASIBILITY_REPORT.md` proving why heavyweight models/clusters are not prematurely activated without measured ROI and real labeled data. |
